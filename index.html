MusicClubBot Backups

MusicClubBot Backups is a private backup utility used to protect MusicClubBot application data.

Purpose

The utility creates encrypted backups of application databases, configuration, and associated files for disaster recovery.

Google Drive Access

Google Drive is used as off-site storage for encrypted backup archives. Access is authorized by the Google account owner and is not offered as a public service.

Security

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive. Encryption credentials are maintained separately from the stored backups.
