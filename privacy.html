MusicClubBot Backups Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Purpose

MusicClubBot Backups is a private utility that stores encrypted application backups in an authorized Google Drive account.

Information Accessed

The utility accesses Google Drive files and folders as permitted by the Google account owner through OAuth authorization.

Use of Information

Google Drive access is used to upload, list, verify, retrieve, and manage encrypted backup archives.

Storage and Security

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted before upload. Encryption credentials are stored separately from the backup archives.

Sharing

Google Drive information accessed by this utility is not sold or used for advertising. The utility is not offered to the public.

Data Retention

Backup archives are retained according to the operator's backup retention policy and may be deleted during routine rotation.

Access Revocation

The account owner may revoke the utility's Google Drive authorization through their Google Account security settings.
